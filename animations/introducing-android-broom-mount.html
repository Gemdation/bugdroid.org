Introducing the Android Broom Mount

About

The video parodies smartphones commercials to show off a broom mount for witches. The spell-proof broom mount is a modern Bugdroid that grabs onto the broom that can work in any condition, though only available in Oz.

This video was promotional material for the 2024 movie adaptation Wicked.
