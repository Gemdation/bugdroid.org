Crowd

About

In this Androidify-styled animation, a little droid brings out a big speaker that sparks a massive crowd. They all dance to This Is How We Do It.

The tagline "be together. not the same." is part of to Android's 2014 brand refresh and the release of the Androidify avatar maker.
