Winnebago

About

In this Androidify-styled animation, a populated van drives by bumping to Whoomp! (There It Is). The name "Winnebago" comes from the RV manufacturer.

The tagline "be together. not the same." is part of Android's 2014 brand refresh and the release of the Androidify avatar maker.
