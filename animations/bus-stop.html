Bus Stop

About

In this Androidify-styled animation, a phone is heard ringing amongst a group of people at a bus stop. After a subtle mystery, a tiny lady brings out a comically large smartphone.

The tagline "be together. not the same." is part of Android's 2014 brand refresh and the release of the Androidify avatar maker.
