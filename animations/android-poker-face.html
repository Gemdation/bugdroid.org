Android Poker Face

About

A droid is practicing his poker faces for a game while his presumable Honeycomb wife admires a talent that he has. The husband assures her that Dragonplay Live has various avatars on then Android Marketplace.

This is a commercial for Dragonplay Live Hold'em, a poker game using Facebook integrations for social features. Bugdroid appears in some of the avatars available, thought the game is now unavaiable on Google Play.
