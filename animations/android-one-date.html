Android One: Date

About

In this Androidify-styled animation, a man is a having a private date but has various relatives at his door. He effortlessly ignores them, but conveniently lets the pizza man deliver to him (metaphor for Android One's priority notifications).

From Google India's channel, this was a promotional video for Android One. Also seen is the Lava Pixel V1 smartphone.

View original YouTube description

Android One devices come equipped with Priority Notifications. So that special moments remain interruption-free. Learn more at www.android.com/one/india.
