About

In this Androidify-styled animation, a presumable father and kids are speeding in a scooter to the hospital. A mother is out and shows them a basket, and then reveals the baby inside.

From Google India's channel, this was a promotional video for Android One. Also seen is the Lava Pixel V1 smartphone, the "baby".
