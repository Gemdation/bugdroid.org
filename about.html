Everything Droid!

This is a Bugdroid fan site but it is first an archive of Bugdroid merchandise. But that doesn't mean it is limited to official merchandise, it also includes fan works too like the games! This website wouldn't be possible without the Internet Archive's help in easy access to these sites, so I'm trying to make this site easier to pick through as well.

Here and there, you will see various references to Android software. One of the more obvious things is the design of this website, the early versions of Android! Most of this website is focused on the classic Android branding. Sorry, Material You!
