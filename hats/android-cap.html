Android Cap

About

Android Cap is a black cap with a white Bugdroid outline on it. The classic wordmark is also on the strap.

This design is similar to the one seen on the 2009 Android T-Shirt.

View original Google Store synopsis

Need a cap to wear with your new Android t-shirt? This twill, 6 panel, structured cap is your answer. The Android logo is embroidered in white on the side and the cap also features an adjustable Velcro closure with the Android logo embroidered on the strap. Cap comes in black with white Android.
