Standard

About

The Standard figure is classic Bugdroid with poseable arms and a wordmark on its behind, setting the standard for the rest of the figurines.

This was and is still the longest base model for all of the Android figurines by Dead Zebra, being around for 11 years.

3" vinyl figure with rotating head and arms.

One standard edition plain old green Android figure. It is in the same packaging as the rest of the series, but you actually know what you are getting!
