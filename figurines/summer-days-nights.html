Summer Days & Nights Set

About

The Summer Days & Nights Set contains two retro party-going Bugdroids. The set is inspired by the 1970's and 1980's and contains two figures and a total of 4 accessories.

View original Dead Zebra synopsis

Summer is here and it's time to hit the beach for some fun in the sun and then cool off with some refreshing beverages at night! This 70's and 80's inspired set includes a classic retro Summer Days beach goer complete with shades and a beachball, and a chill jacket-wearing Summer Nights Android complete with refreshing iced tea and the requisite "sunglasses at night".

  • 2 x 3¼" Vinyl figures.
  • Poseable arms, head and antennae.
  • Teeny tiny sunglasses!
  • Beach ball & Iced tea glass accessories.
  • Windowed display box.
