Series 1

About

The Series 1 contains various stylized Bugdroids. This was the first entry in a line of Dead Zebra's Android figurine series.

Most of the designs are credited to Dead Zebra, Inc. Exceptions are the Standard Green figure with Google, and the Creature and Darknet with Andrew Bell

View original Dead Zebra Shop synopsis

Full Set: 3" vinyl figure with rotating head and arms.

Each figure features one of 12 different random designs. Each case contains 16 total figures including at least one rare secret figure!

APK 2008: A tribute to the famous HAL 9000, APK is an Android file format, and 2008 refers to the date of Android’s first public debut.

Copper Wash: Never formally named, often referred to as “Rust”, “Rusty” or “Copper Wash”
