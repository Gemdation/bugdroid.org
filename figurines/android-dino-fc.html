Android x Dino FC

About

The Android x Dino FC figure is a modern Bugdroid in a full Chrome Dino FC kit alongside a football. (US/CA: Soccer ball)

This release was part of the larger Chrome Dino FC series on the worldwide Google Merch Shop, a series themed around the FIFA World Cup.
