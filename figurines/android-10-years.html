Android 10 Years

About

The Android 10 Years figurine line were designed for Android's 10th anniversary. Designs include the Astronaut, Cake, Runner, and Varisty.

For unknown reasons, the Runner is not acknowledged on the Sold Out page of shop.deadzebra.com, while the other members of the 10 Years line are.
