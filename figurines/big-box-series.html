Big Box Series

About

Big Box Series contains various stylized Bugdroids, not to be confused with Series 01. This set contained miscellaneous styles to be sold to retail.

View original Dead Zebra blog synopsis

This open edition features simple but fun designs in visible window boxes and was originally produced for large North American retailers including Urban Outfitters, FRYS, FYE and Barnes & Noble. They are not considered part of the limited edition designer series or special editions.
