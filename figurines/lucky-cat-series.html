Lucky Cat Series

About

The Lucky Cat Series is a collection of various Japanese lucky cats, they vary from personal to businesses. The entire set is designed by Shane Jessup and all figurines appear to have at least one fixed-position arm.

The case is maroon-colored with a golden Catdroid.
