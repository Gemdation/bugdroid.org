Year of the Horse

About

The Year of the Horse figure is a modern Bugdroid with Lunar New Year attire alongside golden rope belt. It has a hobby horse that can have different heads depending on luck.

The figure is a successor to the 2014 Year of the Horse figure. Instead of a heavily decorated red horse, this one just has an outfit with accessories.

View original Dead Zebra synopsis

Each ornately decorated Android figure includes one of three mystery hobby horse accessories representing a different 'fortune' for the new year!

  • Brown (5 in 10): Compassion & Skill
  • Grey (4 in 10): Stability & Balance
  • Gold (1 in 10): Accomplishment & Wisdom.

What will your new year hold?

  • 3¼" Vinyl figure.
  • Poseable arms, head and antennae.
  • One of three possible hobby horse accessory head colors.
  • Golden rope belt.
