Playtime

About

The Playtime figure is a white customizable Bugdroid figurine made for the release of (the now defunct) Google Jamboard.

Unlike the original D.I.Y., this one has fixed arms and head. It also comes with dry-erase markers and an eraser. It was a Google-exclusive figurine.
