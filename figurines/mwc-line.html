Mobile World Congress 2012

About

At the 2012 Mobile World Congress, a line of transparent colurful figurines were given out. Each figurine has a head that can be popped off, letting items get stored inside.

Colors came in green, blue, red, yellow, and purple. These are technically the predecessor to the styles seen later in the Rainbow Set.
