Glow in the Dark

About

The Glow in the Dark figure is white Bugdroid that, with its environment darkened, will glow with a modern green.

Like its original model, it is marked up on the US Google Merch Shop, being at $21 instead of $14. The worldwide Google Merch Shop price (£23) stayed the same.
