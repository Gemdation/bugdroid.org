Year of the Snake Set

About

The Year of the Snake Set contains snake whispering-modern Bugdroids next to snakes styled after those in the Google Snake mini game. It has a total of 4 figurines in total.

When this item was upcoming it uniquely said "SSSOON", and when it was sold out it said "SSSSSOLD OUT". This set was also the first to use the modern Bugdroids.
