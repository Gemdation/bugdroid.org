Circus Friends

About

The Circus Friends set includes a colorful cast of bugdroid circus members. When buying a full set, a cardboard backdrop is included alongside a chance to get the Dancing Lion figurine.

This was the last set to use the Iconic-styled bugdroids.

Step right up! Come witness the most spectacular display! Join our Circus Friends as they seek to amaze & entertain. A new set of Android mini collectibles featuring classic circus performers, costumed animal characters & fun accessories. 9 different 3-4" tall individual mystery box designs in each set, with a chance to take home a rare bear!
