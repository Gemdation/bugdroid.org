Google Executive Set

About

The Google Executive Set figures are dressed as Google executives, complete with suit and glasses.

It was a Google-exclusive figurine designed by Giovanni Calabrese and Dead Zebra, Inc. It was given out at the 2013 Google Executive Summit.
