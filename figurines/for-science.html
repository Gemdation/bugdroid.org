...for Science!

About

...for Science! is a mini series of human-based figurines that used people with great contributions to science. These are Isaac Newton, Nikola Tesla, and Marie Curie alongside accessories that correlate to their work.

While they were all seperate releases, there was a full set option on the Dead Zebra Shop for convenience.

View original Dead Zebra Shop synopsis

Set:
“…for Science!” is a series of Android Mini Collectible special edition figures celebrating some of the greatest scientific minds in history.

Newton:
1642-1726
Newton established formulations for laws of motion and universal gravitation, and developed important theories regarding the nature of light.

  • Figure includes Principia Mathematica book and prism accessories.

Tesla:
(1856-1943)
The multi-talented inventor and engineer was instrumental in the design of the modern alternating current electricity supply system.

  • Figure includes mini Tesla coil accessory.

Curie:
(1867-1934)
Her work included amazing advancements in the field she named “radioactivity,” including the discovery of the elements polonium and radium.

  • Includes mini plant accessory
