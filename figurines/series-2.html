Series 2

About

The Series 2 contains 14 various stylized Bugdroids. This was the second entry in a line of Dead Zebra's Android figurine series, it brought 2 extras in from the last entry.

View original Dead Zebra Shop synopsis

Full Set: 3" vinyl figure with rotating head and arms.

Each figure features one of 12 different random designs. Each case contains 16 total figures including at least one rare secret figure!

  • Random 3" blind box vinyl figure
  • 11 regular designs plus 1 rare secret design & 2 rare variants
  • Sold individually or order 16 for a case!

Cases of 16 include at least 12 different designs (one rare)!
