MetroPCS

About

The MetroPCS figure is a white Bugdroid with its eponymous logo and brand slogan on it. MetroPCS, now Metro by T-Mobile, is an American phone carrier.

Seen on this article, the figure was given out at a MetroPCS event and signed by members of band Far East Movement.
