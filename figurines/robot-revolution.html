Robot Revolution Set

About

The Robot Revolution Set, technically Series 7, of Dead Zebra's artist figurine line contains various stylized Bugdroids with a novelty of mix and match parts between all of the figurines.

The case is cyan-colored with a blue case strip. This case is the last entry in the traditinal artist series.

View original Dead Zebra Blog synopsis

Remix * Reimagine * Rebuild! Inspired by the open nature of their namesake operating system, this new Android mini collectible series takes re-mixable robots to the next level! Featuring artwork from from Google, Andrew Bell, Chuckboy, Patricio Oliver, Loulou & Tummie, Kong Andri and Scott Tolleson. Collect 15 different designs and three variants, or build your own unique combinations!
