Google ADX-RTB

About

The Google ADX-RTB figure is dark green with a single right eye alongside binary code on its back. It is akin to the APK-2008 figure seen in Series 1.

It was a Google-exclusive figurine designed by Dead Zebra, Inc. for a promotion involving Google's DoubleClick Ad Exchange protocol.
