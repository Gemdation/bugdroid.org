Year of the Dragon Set

About

The 2024 Year of the Dragon Set contains four of the modern Bugdroids celebrating the Year of the Dragon. The set includes an accordion dragon and a pearl staff.

This set notably released before the Standard 2023 figure was, similar to the Work From Home and Standard 2019.
