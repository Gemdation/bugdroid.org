Series 6

About

The Series 6 of Dead Zebra's artist figurine line contains 16 various stylized Bugdroids. The case is purple-colored with a blue case strip.

This case was the last to use the traditional numbers.

The new series of 3″ collectibles features 16 designs from some amazingly talented artists. Andrew Bell, Andrea Kang, Colus, Dwaine Morris, Igor Ventura, Jessica Wang, Nathan Jurevicius and Otto Bjornik all lend their talents to the series!
