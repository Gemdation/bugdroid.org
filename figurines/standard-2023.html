Standard Edition 2023

About

The 2023 Standard Edition figure (referred to as Green) is of the modern Bugdroid with movable antennas and arms. As it is based off of the 3D model, it is a nearly indenticial to the logo.

It is interestingly marked up on the US Google Merch Shop, being at $18 instead of $14. Even more in the worldwide Google Merch Shop (£23) due to shipping costs.
