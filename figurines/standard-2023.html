Standard Edition 2023

About

The 2023 Standard Edition figure (referred to as Green) is of the modern Bugdroid with movable antennas and arms. Unlike the previous Bugdroids, this one is a near perfect replica of its logo.

It is interestingly marked up on the US Google Merch Shop, being at $18 instead of $14. Even more in the worldwide Google Merch Shop (£23) due to shipping costs.
