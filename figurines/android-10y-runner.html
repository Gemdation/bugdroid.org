Android 10Y Runner

About

The 10 Years Runner figure is a Bugdroid wearing a marathon shirt made for Android's 10th anniversary. The shirt has the years Android has been in the public starting with 2008, the year version 1.0 was released.

Interestingly, the Runner is not acknowledged on the Sold Out page of shop.deadzebra.com, while the other members of the 10Y set are.
