Series 4

About

The Series 4 Set of Dead Zebra's artist figurine line contains 15 various stylized Bugdroids and was the first in the series to include a human-styled figure.

The case is navy-colored with a brown Bugdroid and also had a misprinted series labal.

View original Dead Zebra synopsis

The artists series returns for series 4 with some of the most ambitious designs to date! 15 different designs including two rare variants and one secret chase figure!

Note: Due to a goof on our end, these say Series 3 in the legal and sku text on the bottom of the display case! Oops!
