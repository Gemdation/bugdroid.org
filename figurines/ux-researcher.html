UX Researcher

About

The UX Researcher figure is a classic Bugdroid dressed as a computer scientist. Alongside its glasses, "I help Google focus on the user." is written on the back of the coat.

The artist is of this Google-exclusive figurine Prakash Chandran and was given to researchers who made significant contributions.
