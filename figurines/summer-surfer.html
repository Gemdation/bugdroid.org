Summer Surfer

About

The Summer Surfer set includes a green-pink Bugdroid with a surfboarf and wave accessory. The figurine uniquely has magnetic feet to stick to the surfboard. The set also came with an adhesive set for the wave figure.

Surfs up, friends! Grab your board and hit the beach with the new Summer Surfer edition. Our sun-kissed friend comes with board and a special springy desktop wobble wave to ride on! Watch it Wobble!
