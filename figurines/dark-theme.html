Dark Theme

About

The Dark Theme figure is of a recolored Standard Edition 2019, but in dark mode! The body is recolored to a dark navy and the eyes and mouth rim glow in the dark.

This glowing recolor is the predecessor to the newer Glow in the Dark figure.
