a-MAZE-ing

About

a-MAZE-ing is a transparent figure that has a green maze inside with a tiny silver marble inside.

Interestingly, the worldwide Google Merch Shop refers to it as Android Puzzle Bot Figurine, a potentially unaware call back to the actual Puzzlebot.
