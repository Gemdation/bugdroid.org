Standard D.I.Y. MEGA Edition

About

The Standard D.I.Y. MEGA Edition figure is a large white Bugdroid that can open up to reveal an insulating soda container. Unlike the original Standard MEGA Edition, this one has removable limbs for ease of decorating.

View original Dead Zebra Shop synopsis

Show off your creative chops on a grand scale with a blank massive MEGA edition ready for your artistic touch. A huge figure to paint up and plenty of room on the inside to fit electronics for your more ambitious projects.

  • 9¾" tall, 8½" wide Vinyl and ABS!
  • Poseable ball jointed arms can be popped off for painting
  • Removable head.
  • Insulated internal chamber.

Product is an adult collectible and not intended for children under the age of 15! Figure designed in New York by Andrew Bell, made in China, produced by Dyzplastic.
