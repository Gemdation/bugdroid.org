Dancing Lion

About

The Dancing Lion figurine is a red Bugdroid lion with a scroll and lettuce. While representing Chinese New Year 2013, it is said to span into Korean and Japanese culture as well.

It is noticably not a snake for its yearly zodiac as the lion spans more into the culture instead of just certain years.
