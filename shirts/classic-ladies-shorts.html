Classic Ladies Shorts

About

The Classic Ladies Shorts are mainly black with white and green accents. They feature a cycling Bugdroid and are matched with the bike jersey.

View original Google Store synopsis

Complete your collection of cycling gear with a pair of these ladies' Android classic bike shorts. Designed for women by Hincapie Sportswear, they're made from extremely durable 260g Lycra and feature a sublimated back panel and Hincapie Gel-Gripper™ elastic leg openings. The anatomical, 4-way stretch Hincapie Team Chamois and straight printed leg side panels make this a must-have for the competitive or recreational cyclist. This design features an Android cycling with green, grey and black accents. A great complement to the Android bike jersey!
