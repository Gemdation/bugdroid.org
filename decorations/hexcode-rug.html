Hexcode Rug

About

The Hexcode Rug is circle rug of a black Bugdroid with green hexagons. The design of the Bugdroid is matching the design of Series 02's Hexcode figurine.

Later, a similar-looking Bubble Bot rug would be produced.

View original Dead Zebra synopsis

This durable rug based off of the Hexcode Android design looks great on any floor! We even mixed up a custom batch of PMS376 (Android Green) to get it just right.

  • approx 33" diameter dyed nylon
  • ideal for indoor use
  • easy to clean
  • skid resistant vinyl back

These rugs are made in the USA and hand finished so they may vary an inch or so in diameter.
