Dead Zebra's Plushie

About

Dead Zebra's Android plushie is a recreation of the classic Bugdroid bot complete with a moveable head.

In colors red, yellow, blue, and green, possibly a nod to Google's branding colors. It was said to be produced by Commonwealth Toys while also having Dead Zebra's brand on it, possible licensing.
