Dead Zebra's Plushie

About

Dead Zebra's Android plushie is a recreation of the classic Bugdroid bot complete with a moveable head. It was produced by Dead Zebra from 2011 to 2012.

According to a video, these had multiple sizes and colors. 16", 9", 6", and 'backpack clip'. In colors red, yellow, blue, and green, possibly a nod to Google's branding colors.
