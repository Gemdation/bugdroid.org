Google's Flat Plush

About

Google's Flat Plush (unknown real name) is a wide slim plushie based on the classic Bugdroid design, potentially having the most accurate representation of the 2D logo.

It is officially licensed by Google, has a permanent arm pose, and is akin to a plushie pillow. 17".
