Android Mosquito Lamp

About

The Android Mosquito Lamp is a pest control lamp that shocks mosquitos and other insects when they go into contact with the grid. It has a fan inside for possibly temperature control.

An interesting fact about this product in general is that it may be one of the only products made using Bugdroid that isn't directly a computer/mobile accessory.

This is a white label product from FRIC.
