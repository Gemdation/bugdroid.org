Android Adapter Plug

About

The Android Adapter Plug is a Bugdroid-shaped travel-adapting charger. By default, the head is a Type A plug, with the body containing the adapting parts. The body has a button that can disconnect the head for a regular plug and four levers to show the different plug types.

It has regular cartoon eyes instead of regular black dots, moveable arms, and a base cover that can turn it into a stand. From the images, some parts may not be included in the main body due to size (e.g. Type L).

This is a white label product from FRIC.
