Android Ice Cube Tray

About

The Android Ice Cube Tray is flexible silicone tray of eight Bugdroid-shaped molds, it can be used for making ice cubes or other molds.

The ice cube tray itself appears on the FRIC catalog, an implication to where the Android Foundry got this from.

View original Android Foundry synopsis

Waiter, there’s an Android in my drink! Cubes are boring, fill your glass with some miniature robots! This flexible silicon tray can also be used as a chocolate mold!
