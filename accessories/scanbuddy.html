ScanBuddy

About

The ScanBuddy is a mobile barcode scanner accessory made by Generalscan for Android smartphones. The design seems to be based off the white Bugdroid outline look seen on the Nexus One Pouch.

Generalscan had released a companion app for the Scanbuddy in 2014, though the app was later auto-delisted from Google Play in 2024 for being outdated.
