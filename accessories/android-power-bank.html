Android Power Bank

About

The Android Power Bank is a green Bugdroid-shaped portable power bank. It has a USB-A and MicroUSB-A for charging and receiving charger respectively. It comes in an additional 3 colors, yellow, blue, and pink.

This is a white label product from FRIC.
