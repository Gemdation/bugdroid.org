PNY Robot Adapter

About

The PNY Robot Adapter is a Bugdroid-shaped male MicroUSB-B to female USB-A adapter. The adapter is one full piece.

The head folds to reveal the MicroUSB which then can used for USB storage or charging another device. The "Green" part of the name implies that more color variants exist, however have not been found.
