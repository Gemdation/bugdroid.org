Android Mini Speaker

About

The Android Mini Speaker is a speaker modeled off of Bugdroid, it has a speaker on the top of its head and a USB-A and headphone jack.

This design notably tries to mimic Dead Zebra's Standard figurine.

This is a white label product from FRIC.
