About

The 2025 reboot of Androidify is a Bugdroid avatar creator powered by Google Gemini. It was developed by Google employees and released under Android Samples on Google Play.

It was said to be the Gemini 2.5 Flash version but it was likely updated over time. Avatars are made entirely from generative AI via a photo or prompt to use.

The web version can be found at androidify.com.

APKM Notice: Android's standard Package Installer cannot handle APKMs. It is recommended to download from Google Play, or if still needed, use the APKMirror Installer.

View original Google Play Store synopsis

Androidify yourself!
Create, accessorize and share Android characters that look like you, your best friend, your Uncle Sal—anyone, really.

Create and share your animated Android characters as emoticons, animated GIFs and pictures over texts, posts or chats.

Be sure to submit your creations online, where you can share to the Androidify.com Gallery to hang with Android characters from around the world.
