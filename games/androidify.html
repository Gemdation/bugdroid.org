About

Androidify is a Bugdroid avatar creator, various accessories are available to pick from. It was developed by Google and had the feature to submit creations to the (now defunct) androidify.com for a chance to appear in promotional material. These ended up being various animated videos.

View original Google Play Store synopsis

Androidify yourself!
Create, accessorize and share Android characters that look like you, your best friend, your Uncle Sal—anyone, really.

Create and share your animated Android characters as emoticons, animated GIFs and pictures over texts, posts or chats.

Be sure to submit your creations online, where you can share to the Androidify.com Gallery to hang with Android characters from around the world.
