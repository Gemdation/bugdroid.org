About

Android KitKat Challenge is an action game themed around the Android KitKat promotional videos and inspired by the popular Dumb Ways to Die series.

Various mini challenges such as cut a KitKat, spot the Droid with the KitKat, tap something, etc. And while playing the timer will get shorter over time. The game has 3 extra wallpapers and had a Google Play intergration.

View original Google Play Store synopsis

Play with Android and KitKat! Be fast, show your skills and have fun with different challenges. Download the game and get cool wallpapers for your smartphone or tablet and watch the videos from Android KitKat.

Android is the operating system that powers over 1 billion smartphones and tablets. This game celebrates the launch of Android KitKat. Since these devices make our lives so sweet, each Android version is named after a dessert: Cupcake, Donut, Eclair, Froyo, Gingerbread, Honeycomb, Ice Cream Sandwich, and Jelly Bean. As everybody finds it difficult to stay away from chocolate we decided to name the new version of Android after one of our favorite chocolate treats, Kitkat!

Download the game now on your mobile or tablet, and say goodbye to the boring times!
