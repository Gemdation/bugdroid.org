Android Espresso Set

About

Android Espresso Set is a three piece cup set that is shaped as Bugdroid standing on a plate. The set includes the lid (head), cup (body), and plate for it to stand on. The arm pointing out is used as a handle.

This was sold on Etsy under the name Robropresso. The packaging is themed around the design itself and has a sticker of the Robropresso brand.
